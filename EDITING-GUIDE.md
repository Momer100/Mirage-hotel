# Mirage Hotel — Website Editing Guide

A simple, no-jargon guide to updating your own website. You don't need any technical
knowledge — if you can use email, you can do this.

Your website content lives in a friendly editor called **Studio**. You log in, change what
you want, press **Publish**, and the website updates itself.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open your web browser and go to: **https://www.miragehotel.co.uk/studio**
2. Sign in with the account you were invited with (click "Google" or "Email" and use the
   email address the invite was sent to).
3. You're in. You'll see a menu down the left-hand side.

> **First time and it won't let you in?** You need an invite. Ask your web person to add you
> at **sanity.io/manage → your project → Members → Invite**.

---

## 2. The golden rule: **Publish**

Nothing you change goes live until you press the green **Publish** button (bottom of the
screen).

- Type your change → press **Publish**.
- The live website updates within about **1 minute**.
- If you don't see the change on the site yet, wait a moment and **refresh the page twice**
  (press the refresh/reload button in your browser two times). This is normal.

Think of it like this: editing in Studio is like writing a letter; **Publish** is putting it
in the postbox.

---

## 3. Getting around

The left-hand menu is where everything lives:

| Menu item | What's inside |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Site Settings** | Your phone, mobile, email, address, check-in/out times, direct-booking discount |
| **Home Page** | The big headline, the "Welcome" text, and the Director section |
| **Gallery** | All the photos on the Gallery page |
| **Policies** | The "Good to Know" / policy points (check-in, cancellations, etc.) |
| **Rooms** | Each room: its name, description, price, features and photos |

Click any item to open it and start editing.

---

## 4. Editing text and headings

Example — changing a room description:

1. Click **Rooms** in the left menu.
2. Click the room you want (e.g. "Classic Double Room").
3. Click into the **Description** box and edit the words.
4. Press **Publish**.

The same idea works everywhere — **Home Page** for the welcome text and headline,
**Policies** for the policy wording, and so on. Click the field, type, **Publish**.

---

## 5. Changing a room's price

1. Click **Rooms** → choose the room.
2. Find **Price from (£/night)**.
3. Type a number (e.g. `79`) → **Publish**. The site will show **"From £79 / night"**.
4. To go back to no price, **clear the box** (leave it empty) → **Publish**. The site will
   show **"Rates on request"** instead.

---

## 6. Changing or replacing a photo

Every photo is an image field. To swap one out:

1. Open the item that has the photo (a room, the Home Page director photo, etc.).
2. Click the current image. A small menu appears — choose **Replace** (or remove it and
   upload a new one). You can also just **drag a photo file** from your computer onto it.
3. Wait for the upload bar to finish.
4. Fill in the **Alt text** box — a short description of the photo (e.g. "Double room with sea
   view"). This helps Google and visually-impaired guests. **Always add alt text.**
5. Press **Publish**.

> **Tip:** Use good, bright landscape photos where you can. Very large files still work — the
> website automatically shrinks them for fast loading.

---

## 7. The Gallery — add, remove, reorder photos

1. Click **Gallery** in the left menu.
2. You'll see the list of photos under **Gallery photos**.
   - **Add a photo:** click **Add item** → upload the image → add alt text.
   - **Remove a photo:** click the **⋮** (three dots) on a photo → **Remove**.
   - **Reorder:** click and hold the drag handle (the dots on the left of a row) and drag it
     up or down.
3. Press **Publish**.

> **Keep it tidy:** the gallery looks best when the number of photos is a **multiple of 4**
> (e.g. 16, 20, 24). Otherwise the last row may look uneven.

---

## 8. Undo a change / roll back (History)

Made a mistake, or want to go back to how it was? Every change is saved forever, so you can
always roll back.

1. Open the item you want to fix (e.g. **Home Page** or **Site Settings**).
2. Click the **History** button — the little **clock icon** near the top-right of the editor.
3. A timeline of past versions appears on the right. Click a point in the timeline to see how
   things looked at that time.
4. When you find the version you want, click **Restore** (top of that panel).
5. Press **Publish** to make the restored version live.

That's it — you can never permanently lose your old content.

---

## 9. Do's and don'ts

**Do**
- ✅ Always press **Publish** after editing (or nothing changes on the site).
- ✅ Always add **Alt text** to photos.
- ✅ Use **History → Restore** if you want to undo something.

**Don't**
- ❌ Don't change a room's **Slug** field (the little `double`, `triple` code) — it links the
   room to the booking form. Change the name and everything else freely, just not the slug.
- ❌ Don't try to delete **Site Settings, Home Page, Gallery** or **Policies** — they're the
   core of the site. (You *can* edit them all you like.)
- ❌ Adding a brand-new **type** of room (a 5th room type) needs your web person — but you can
   change everything about the 4 existing rooms yourself.

---

## 10. Troubleshooting

**"I published but the website hasn't changed."**
Give it about a minute, then **refresh the live page twice**. Changes are near-instant but the
site briefly caches the old version.

**"I can't log in."**
You need to be invited. Ask your web person to add your email at
**sanity.io/manage → Members → Invite**.

**"I edited something and it looks wrong."**
Use **History → Restore** (Section 8) to go back to the last good version.

---

## 11. Quick cheat-sheet

| I want to… | Do this |
|------------|---------|
| Change a phone number, email, address or check-in time | **Site Settings** → edit → **Publish** |
| Change the homepage headline or welcome text | **Home Page** → edit → **Publish** |
| Update the director's bio or photo | **Home Page** → Director fields → **Publish** |
| Change a room's price | **Rooms** → room → **Price from** → **Publish** |
| Edit a room description or features | **Rooms** → room → edit → **Publish** |
| Swap a room photo | **Rooms** → room → click image → Replace → alt text → **Publish** |
| Add / remove / reorder gallery photos | **Gallery** → Add item / drag / Remove → **Publish** |
| Edit a policy | **Policies** → edit item → **Publish** |
| Undo a change | Open the item → **History** (clock) → pick a version → **Restore** → **Publish** |

---

*And remember the golden rule: **Publish**, then refresh the live page twice. Happy editing!*
